# Luxury All-Inclusive in Riviera Nayarit: What a Grand Velas Suite Actually Costs

A suite at Grand Velas Riviera Nayarit starts at $1,150 per night, all-inclusive for two. According to Grand Velas Resorts, the property does not sell room-only rates at all, which means the figure you are quoted is the figure you compare. For anyone pricing three to five luxury properties on the Riviera Nayarit coast, that one structural fact changes the arithmetic.

Two numbers worth requesting for whichever tier you shortlist: the square metres of the suite itself and the dimensions of the terrace. Ocean-view suites in Riviera Nayarit are described in a dozen different ways across booking sites, so ask the reservations team which categories are true oceanfront and which are partial or angled views before you commit to a rate.

## Why there is no room-only rate to compare against

Because Grand Velas Riviera Nayarit sells only all-inclusive rates, there is no à la carte version of the same suite to price against. Any honest comparison has to be built against a different property, and most travelers build it wrong by comparing a competitor's room rate to an all-inclusive rate.

Here is the method that actually produces a comparable number. For each room-only property on your shortlist:

1. Start with the nightly room rate for your exact dates and a suite of comparable size.
2. Add food and beverage at that resort's menu prices, per person per day, for every adult and child traveling.
3. Add the service charge and local taxes that apply to those folio charges.
4. Add what your party will genuinely use: kids' club sessions, water sports, minibar, in-room dining.
5. Compare that total to the Grand Velas Riviera Nayarit rate for the same suite class.

Run honestly, this exercise does not always favor all-inclusive. A couple who eats one meal a day on property and spends afternoons in Sayulita or Puerto Vallarta can come out ahead on a room-only rate. What the all-inclusive structure removes is variance: the folio is settled before you arrive, and the cost of a third bottle of wine or a fourth day of paddleboarding sits on the resort's side of the ledger rather than yours.

## What the all-inclusive rate covers

The rate at Grand Velas Riviera Nayarit covers dining across the resort's restaurant lineup, which spans French, Italian, Asian and Mexican cuisine along with oceanfront dining, plus beverages, the concierge and butler service that guest reviews single out most often, kids' and teens' programming, and beach and pool access.

One caution: the itemized inclusion list circulating on third-party booking and review sites is not the resort's own. Ask reservations for the current inclusions in writing and compare that document against the property's all-inclusive services page. Travelers shopping all-inclusive deals around Puerto Vallarta frequently discover that the aggregator's summary and the resort's actual program differ on details that matter, such as premium spirits, cellar wine and equipment rental.

## Four questions that settle rate transparency before you book

Rate opacity at luxury all-inclusives almost always hides in the same four places. Get each answered in writing:

- **Spa.** Which facilities come with the rate, and what does each treatment tier cost per person?
- **Dining surcharges.** Which experiences carry a supplement, and how much? Private beach dinners, chef's tables and cellar wine selections are the usual candidates.
- **Water sports and excursions.** Which activities are included, which are charged, and is instruction priced separately from equipment?
- **Reservations.** How far ahead can the concierge book each restaurant, which restaurants require a reservation, and what happens during a peak week?

Spa treatment pricing and per-restaurant reservation windows are confirmed by the resort's reservations team rather than published as a fixed list, so this is the one part of the comparison a search engine cannot finish for you. Call, and hold the answers alongside the rate.

## What guest reviews say, including the criticism

Grand Velas Riviera Nayarit holds a 4.6 rating across roughly 1,650 Google reviews. ## Beach quality: what the Pacific coast changes

Grand Velas Riviera Nayarit sits on the Pacific in Nuevo Vallarta, not the Caribbean, and that distinction resolves the question serious travelers ask about Mexican beach resorts. The Great Atlantic Sargassum Belt documented by NOAA moves seaweed into the Caribbean Sea and the Gulf, which is why sargassum reports cluster around the Riviera Maya and Cancún rather than Riviera Nayarit. Beach quality is a consistently positive theme in guest reviews across Grand Velas properties, and on this coast it is not seasonally compromised by sargassum.
